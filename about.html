BORN IN THE SHADOWS

Welcome to the cult. We are a Puerto Rican Owned and operated home-based café rooted deeply in the heart of Philadelphia.

What started as an obsession with the perfect, dark roast has evolved into an exclusive, small-batch coffee experience. We operate from the shadows, prioritizing quality and unique, curated aesthetics over commercial volume. From our signature Flan de Queso lattes to our classic Coquito blends, every cup is crafted with authentic flavor and a dark, cinematic twist.
